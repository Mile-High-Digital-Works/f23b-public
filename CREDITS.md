Releases through v1.4 used the Grinnelli Designs / Branden Hooper F-22A adaptation; their source and notices remain with those releases.

The revised slate and MALICE motor C are included. Weapon values are DCS game choices.

The Hornet supplies the pilot cockpit from the user's installation. The F-35
insert and eight V11 maps are absent. Historical evaluation content is absent
from the public runtime; earlier releases retain their own licence records.
