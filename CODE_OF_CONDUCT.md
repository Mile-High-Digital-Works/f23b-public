# Code of conduct

The F-23B community welcomes players and contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels.
These rules apply to project issues, pull requests, and other project discussions.
They also apply when someone represents the project in another community.

## Expected behavior

- Treat others with respect.
- Help new players and contributors.
- Give clear, useful feedback about the work.
- Respect different opinions and experiences.
- Accept corrections and take responsibility for mistakes.
- Keep discussions relevant to the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Sexual attention or content that others do not want.
- Publication of another person's private information without permission.
- Spam, impersonation, or repeated disruption of discussions.
- Retaliation against someone who reports a concern.

## Report a concern

Use GitHub's **Report content** option on the relevant comment or account to report abuse privately.
See [GitHub's reporting guide](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).
GitHub Support receives these reports. They are not private messages to the project maintainer.
Do not put personal information or sensitive allegations in a public issue.

For project moderation, mention [@Mile-High-Digital-Works](https://github.com/Mile-High-Digital-Works) in the relevant discussion when it is safe to do so.
The maintainer can remove content, close or lock discussions, and limit participation.
Serious or repeated violations can result in a permanent block from the repository.
Moderation considers the behavior, its impact, and previous violations.
These rules also apply to maintainers.
