F-23B

Name: F-23B Black Widow II
Type: Community aircraft
Crew: 1

Requirements:
DCS World 2.9.31.29434 on Windows
Installed and activated DCS: F/A-18C Hornet

Cockpit and systems:
The pilot uses the installed F/A-18C Hornet cockpit and avionics.
The exterior is a licensed YF-23 model with project changes.
Flight forces are provided by the F-23B flight bridge.
Native systems and sound use the installed Hornet.

Armament:
Internal gun
AIM-9X Block II on the two outward-swinging bay doors
Three AIM-424 MALICE or AIM-120C missiles in the main bay
Quick Start missions carry two AIM-9X Block II and three MALICE missiles.

Installation:
Copy the ZIP contents into Saved Games/DCS, or use setup for the same files.
DCS game files are not changed.
See the download's F-23B-docs/INSTALL.md for the complete instructions.

Limitations:
Single-player. VR and multiplayer are not yet tested.
Block II post-launch datalink and lock-on after launch are not implemented.
Performance is a simulator design approximation, not measured YF-23 flight data.
